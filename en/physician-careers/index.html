The project

A new family medicine practice in the heart of Côte-des-Neiges

Centre Médical S.A.M. (Société d’Avancement Médical) is an interdisciplinary health centre under development at Plaza Côte-des-Neiges. It will bring together family medicine and RAMQ-covered walk-in consultations, along with other health services to be added as recruitment progresses.

Joining us now means having a say: your preferences on schedules, how consultations are organized and the tools you work with can be built in from the start.

The centre is under development. Items marked “Planned” are part of our plan and will be confirmed during our discussions.

FAQ

Your questions, answered

What types of physicians are you looking for?

Our priority is family physicians, newly graduated or in practice. We are also open to conversations with specialists and other health professionals interested in interdisciplinary practice.

Do you welcome newly graduated physicians?

Yes, and we encourage them to contact us. Orientation to the EMR, billing and clinic processes is planned to ease the transition from residency to practice.

Do I need a PTEM avis de conformité?

To practise family medicine and bill the RAMQ, a physician generally needs an avis de conformité under the plan territorial des effectifs médicaux (PTEM) for their place of practice, issued by the Département territorial de médecine de famille (DTMF). The centre is located in the Côte-des-Neiges–Métro–Parc-Extension RLS. For PTEM 2027, the initial application window is expected to run from December 1 to 15, 2026; please confirm official dates with Santé Québec. We are happy to discuss your situation, but we have no role in granting avis.

What practice arrangements are offered?

Family medicine with patient follow-up and walk-in consultations, covered by the RAMQ. The mix of activities, schedule and agreement are set individually with each physician.

Can I practise part-time?

Yes, part-time practice can be considered, in line with the obligations tied to your avis de conformité, including the required share of billing days in the sub-territory.

What administrative services will be provided?

Reception, appointment booking, schedule management, billing support, supply management, IT support and operational coordination. The detailed list will be presented during our discussions.

What support is planned for RAMQ billing?

Administrative billing support is planned: process orientation, assistance and claim follow-up, according to agreed arrangements. Physicians remain responsible for their billing.

What equipment will be available?

Examination rooms will be equipped for primary care. The equipment list is being finalized; it will be presented during our discussions and your needs can be taken into account.

Will there be an electronic medical record?

Yes. The EMR solution will be confirmed before opening, and every physician will receive an orientation.

Does the clinic have parking?

Yes, Plaza Côte-des-Neiges offers underground parking. Access arrangements for physicians will be confirmed.

What stage is the project at?

The 13-room centre is under development at Plaza Côte-des-Neiges. We are recruiting our first physicians now, so they can help establish the practice.

When does the clinic expect to welcome its first patients?

The opening date has not yet been set. It will be shared with physicians as soon as it is confirmed, then announced on this site.

How are office fees and financial arrangements determined?

They are presented and discussed individually, based on your type of practice and your goals. Our commitment: clear arrangements, explained before any commitment.

Can I discuss the opportunity before applying?

Yes. A first conversation with our management team lets you ask all your questions. No CV is needed to get started.
