Société d’Avancement Médical

An interdisciplinary health centre in Montréal

Centre Médical S.A.M. is an interdisciplinary health centre in Montréal. At the core of our services: family medicine and walk-in consultations covered by the RAMQ, subject to eligibility, supported by a team of physicians, nurse practitioners, psychologists, physiotherapists, pharmacists and other health professionals.

Our approach: accessible, coordinated care for patients, and a practice environment that lets professionals focus fully on medicine.

Our mission

Bringing the supply of care in line with the needs of the population

In Québec, more than one million people still do not have access to a family doctor. This gap between the demand for care and its supply leads to longer wait times, added pressure on emergency departments and medical follow-up that is too often interrupted. Centre Médical S.A.M.’s mission is to help close this gap in concrete terms.

  1. The challenge

    Insufficient access to care

    The shortage of physicians and primary care professionals limits access to regular follow-up and timely care.

  2. Our response

    An interdisciplinary approach

    Bringing together family physicians, nurse practitioners, psychologists, physiotherapists, pharmacists and other health professionals to increase care capacity and optimize patient care.

  3. Our commitment

    Accessible, high-quality care

    Providing family medicine and walk-in services covered by the RAMQ, subject to eligibility, in a simple, human and coordinated care pathway.

Our interdisciplinary team

All essential care, under one roof

Our model brings together the most sought-after primary care professions to give patients a simple, fast and coordinated care pathway.

  • Family physicians

    The heart of the centre: patient care, follow-up and walk-in consultations.

  • Nurse practitioners (IPS)

    Primary care diagnosis, treatment and follow-up, in collaboration with physicians.

  • Nurses

    Assessment, care, vaccination, sample collection and health education.

  • Psychologists and psychotherapists

    Access to mental health services, a priority for many families.

  • Physiotherapists

    Rehabilitation, musculoskeletal pain and return to activity.

  • Pharmacists

    Medication management, chronic disease follow-up and patient counselling.

  • Dietitians

    Support for diabetes, cardiovascular disease and healthy lifestyle habits.

  • Occupational therapists and kinesiologists

    Independence, prevention and rehabilitation through physical activity.

  • Social workers

    Psychosocial support and referral to community resources.

  • Medical specialists

    Targeted consultations based on needs identified by our primary care team.

  • Administrative staff

    Reception, coordination and support to free up clinical time.

Accessibility

Accessible care with the RAMQ

Our medical centre offers medical consultations covered by the Régie de l’assurance maladie du Québec (RAMQ), in accordance with applicable terms.

RAMQQuébec Health Insurance
  • Medical consultations covered subject to eligibility
  • Family medicine and walk-in care

Centre Médical S.A.M. is an independent clinic. It is not affiliated with the Régie de l’assurance maladie du Québec or the Government of Québec. Some professional services (psychology, physiotherapy, etc.) may not be covered by the RAMQ.
