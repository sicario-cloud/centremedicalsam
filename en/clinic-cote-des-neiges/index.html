Medical clinic in Côte-des-NeigesCentre Médical S.A.M.

Centre Médical S.A.M. will open its first 13-room centre in the heart of Côte-des-Neiges, Montréal. An interdisciplinary medical clinic where family medicine and walk-in consultations, covered by the RAMQ subject to eligibility, are supported by a team of health professionals, all under one roof.

The exact address and opening hours will be announced on this site.

Services

All essential care, close to home

For residents of Côte-des-Neiges and surrounding areas (Snowdon, Notre-Dame-de-Grâce, Outremont, Mount Royal and Parc-Extension), one place to be seen, followed and receive coordinated care.

  • Family medicine

    Ongoing care, follow-up and front-line services.

  • Walk-in consultations

    For health problems that can’t wait, covered by the RAMQ subject to eligibility.

  • Nurse practitioners (NPs)

    Diagnosis, treatment and follow-up, in collaboration with physicians.

  • Mental health

    Psychologists and psychotherapists, a priority for many families.

  • Physiotherapy and rehabilitation

    Musculoskeletal pain, occupational therapy, kinesiology and return to activity.

  • Pharmacy

    Medication management and chronic disease follow-up.

  • Nutrition

    Diabetes, cardiovascular disease and healthy lifestyle habits.

  • Medical aesthetics

    Non-surgical aesthetic treatments, provided in a safe medical setting.

Available services and their terms will be confirmed at opening.

Accessibility

Accessible care with the RAMQ

Our medical centre offers medical consultations covered by the Régie de l’assurance maladie du Québec (RAMQ), in accordance with applicable terms.

RAMQQuébec Health Insurance
  • Medical consultations covered subject to eligibility
  • Family medicine and walk-in care

Centre Médical S.A.M. is an independent clinic. It is not affiliated with the Régie de l’assurance maladie du Québec or the Government of Québec. Some professional services (psychology, physiotherapy, etc.) may not be covered by the RAMQ.

FAQ

The Côte-des-Neiges clinic at a glance

Where will the clinic be located?

In the heart of Côte-des-Neiges, Montréal. The exact address will be announced on this site as soon as it is confirmed.

Will there be walk-in consultations?

Yes. Centre Médical S.A.M. will offer family medicine and walk-in consultations covered by the RAMQ, subject to eligibility. Details will be confirmed at opening.

Which neighbourhoods will the clinic serve?

Côte-des-Neiges and surrounding areas, including Snowdon, Notre-Dame-de-Grâce, Outremont, Mount Royal and Parc-Extension.

When will the clinic open?

Opening soon. The date will be announced on this site and on our LinkedIn page.

Are psychology and physiotherapy covered by the RAMQ?

Not necessarily. Some professional services (psychology, physiotherapy, etc.) may not be covered by the RAMQ.
